KuumLight

KuumLight is a small fork of Artemis Android v20.2.6, which is itself a fork of Moonlight Android, the game-streaming client for Sunshine hosts. I made it in September 2026, and it changes how trackpad and mouse input behave.

Trackpad movement and scrolling now stop when your fingers lift, without simulated flick momentum. Mouse back and forward side buttons are forwarded to the host by default. Tap-to-click, two-finger right-click, scrolling, and separate X and Y sensitivity still work. It installs as a separate app, so it can sit next to Artemis.

Status: released as an ARM64 APK (v20.2.6-kuumlight.1). GPL-3.0, and not an official Moonlight or Artemis release. Repository and release.
